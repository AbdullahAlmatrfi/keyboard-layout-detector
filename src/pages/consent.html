KLD Terms of Use and Privacy

This extension works in local-only mode by default. You can still use all keyboard correction features without sharing data.

What always stays local

  • Shortcut correction (Ctrl+Q / Ctrl+Alt / Ctrl+Shift+Q / Ctrl+Z) runs on your device only.
  • No page input text is sent during normal correction.

What can leave your browser and why

  • Only when you click Send Feedback or submit a report, selected data is sent to improve dictionary accuracy and fix bugs.

Your choice

You need to accept the Terms of Use to activate the extension.
