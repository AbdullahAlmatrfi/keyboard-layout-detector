⌨️ Keyboard Layout Detector

Wrong keyboard?
One shortcut fixes it.

Accidentally typed in the wrong language? This extension detects layout mistakes and converts them instantly — Arabic ↔ English.

Press Ctrl + Alt inside any text field to scan & fix
How it works
Three things the extension does
⚡

Auto-Convert

Words typed in the wrong layout that have a clear correct conversion are fixed automatically and instantly.

🟠

Flag Unknown Words

Words that map to something not in the dictionary get an orange box. You can click it to add them.

✅

Leave Clean Text Alone

Intentionally bilingual sentences, URLs, numbers — the extension detects and skips them.

↩️

Undo Anytime

Made a wrong fix? Press Ctrl+Z immediately after to revert to the original text.
